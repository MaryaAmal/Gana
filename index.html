Our manifesto

Pressed for people to enjoy.

We believe freshness begins at the source. With sugarcane grown on farms, carefully selected, and pressed for people to enjoy. We want that connection to remain visible, in the stories we share, the materials we choose, and the experiences we create.

We bring the feeling of sugarcane into your hands through texture, into our spaces through form, and into the experience through the sound of the first press.

We believe sustainability belongs in everyday decisions: where ingredients come from, how products are packaged, and what happens after the drink is finished.

GANA brings these ideas together in a simple, refreshing experience, rooted in the ingredient.

Our purpose

To bring fresh sugarcane juice into everyday life while connecting people to its source through thoughtful design and sensory experiences.

Strategic pillars of experience

Five ideas, one fresh experience.

01

Freshness and Wellness

Fresh sugarcane juice is at the center of GANA. We support a thoughtful approach to everyday refreshment, with clear information about the product and its ingredients.

02

Connection to the Source

The farm story connects the finished drink to where it begins. Named farm sourcing and stories about the ingredient make this connection specific and understandable.

03

Sustainability in Practice

Sustainability guides our choices across sourcing, packaging, and disposal: practical materials, less unnecessary packaging, and care for what happens after the drink is finished.

04

Sensory Connection

Feel the Cane brings its texture into the packaging and our spaces. First Press connects the sound of preparation to the product. Scent and taste extend the experience in store.

05

A Flexible, Recognizable Identity

Our logo, core typography, core colors, and graphic language keep GANA recognizable across flavors, locations, seasons, and applications.
