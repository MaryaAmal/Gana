The brand

Freshly pressed sugarcane juice.

The product focuses on freshness rather than positioning sugarcane juice as another mass-produced soft drink.

GANA is a fresh sugarcane juice brand built around freshness, wellness, and sustainability. It brings sugarcane closer to everyday life through thoughtful packaging, welcoming spaces, and a direct connection to the ingredient.

The identity takes inspiration from sugarcane itself: its green color, long vertical form, and natural texture. These qualities connect the logo, packaging, storefront, and sensory experiences, making every part feel like GANA.

Brand manifesto

We believe freshness begins at the source.

With sugarcane grown on farms, carefully selected, and pressed for people to enjoy.

We want that connection to remain visible, in the stories we share, the materials we choose, and the experiences we create.

We bring the feeling of sugarcane into your hands through texture, into our spaces through form, and into the experience through the sound of the first press.

We believe sustainability belongs in everyday decisions: where ingredients come from, how products are packaged, and what happens after the drink is finished.

GANA brings these ideas together in a simple, refreshing experience, rooted in the ingredient.

Our purpose

To bring fresh sugarcane juice into everyday life while connecting people to its source through thoughtful design and sensory experiences.

Audience

For people who care what they drink.

GANA is for people who care about what they drink and how it is made. They value freshness, wellness, and more thoughtful choices in their daily routines.

This includes students, working professionals, and people looking for convenient refreshment, whether they already enjoy sugarcane juice or are trying it for the first time.

Positioning

GANA positions sugarcane juice as a fresh, contemporary beverage with an elevated yet approachable experience.

The brand brings together farm sourcing, sugarcane-inspired packaging, and sensory design. Its connection to the ingredient extends from the drink to the bottle, store, and digital experience.

Strategic pillars of the experience

Five ideas, one fresh experience.

01

Freshness and Wellness

Fresh sugarcane juice is at the center of GANA. The brand supports a thoughtful approach to everyday refreshment, with clear information about the product and its ingredients.

02

Connection to the Source

The farm story connects the finished drink to where it begins. Named farm sourcing and stories about the ingredient help make this connection specific and understandable.

03

Sustainability in Practice

Sustainability guides choices across sourcing, packaging, and disposal. The focus is on practical material choices, reducing unnecessary packaging, and considering what happens after the drink is finished.

04

Sensory Connection

Sugarcane shapes how people experience GANA. “Feel the Cane” brings its texture into the packaging and physical environment. “First Press” connects the sound of preparation to the product. Scent and taste extend the experience within the store.

05

A Flexible, Recognizable Identity

GANA remains recognizable through its logo, core typography, core colors, and graphic language. These elements support variation across flavors, locations, seasons, and applications while keeping the brand connected.

The experience

Feel it. Hear it. Taste it.

Packaging

Packaging brings “Feel the Cane” directly into people's hands. The tall cylindrical bottle and its raised texture translate the form and surface of sugarcane into an everyday object.

A uniform diameter, a flush cap of the same diameter, and softly chamfered edges keep the bottle recognizable, with the silver GANA wordmark and clear product information.

Feel the Cane

“Feel the Cane” is GANA's main tactile expression. The texture helps people recognize the brand through physical contact as well as appearance.

The bottle is the primary point of contact. Counters, door handles, carriers, and selected surfaces extend the same idea into the wider experience.

First Press

“First Press” connects the sound of preparation to the product. Cane goes in, juice comes out, and you watch it happen, so the connection between the ingredient and the drink stays visible.

Scent & taste

Scent supports the connection to the ingredient and the freshness of the store experience. A floor-standing diffuser in GANA Green sits near the service area, kept subtle so it rests comfortably alongside the aroma of preparation and the drink itself.

Texture

Inspired by the vertical ridges of sugarcane.

Texture is a core part of GANA's identity. It connects the bottle, storefront, counter, and door handles, creating a consistent relationship between the ingredient and its surroundings.
