The brand

Freshly pressed sugarcane juice.

The product focuses on freshness rather than positioning sugarcane juice as another mass-produced soft drink.

GANA is a fresh sugarcane juice brand built around freshness, wellness, and sustainability. It brings sugarcane closer to everyday life through thoughtful packaging, welcoming spaces, and a direct connection to the ingredient.

The identity takes inspiration from sugarcane itself: its green color, long vertical form, and natural texture. These qualities connect the logo, packaging, storefront, and sensory experiences, making every part feel like GANA.

Brand manifesto

We believe freshness begins at the source.

With sugarcane grown on farms, carefully selected, and pressed for people to enjoy. We want that connection to remain visible, in the stories we share, the materials we choose, and the experiences we create.

We bring the feeling of sugarcane into your hands through texture, into our spaces through form, and into the experience through the sound of the first press.

We believe sustainability belongs in everyday decisions: where ingredients come from, how products are packaged, and what happens after the drink is finished. GANA brings these ideas together in a simple, refreshing experience, rooted in the ingredient.

Our purpose

To bring fresh sugarcane juice into everyday life while connecting people to its source through thoughtful design and sensory experiences.

Audience

For people who care what they drink.

GANA is for people who care about what they drink and how it is made. They value freshness, wellness, and more thoughtful choices in their daily routines.

This includes students, working professionals, and people looking for convenient refreshment, whether they already enjoy sugarcane juice or are trying it for the first time.

  • Students
  • Working professionals
  • Convenient refreshment
  • Sugarcane fans
  • First-time tasters

Positioning

GANA positions sugarcane juice as a fresh, contemporary beverage with an elevated yet approachable experience.

The brand brings together farm sourcing, sugarcane-inspired packaging, and sensory design. Its connection to the ingredient extends from the drink to the bottle, store, and digital experience.

Brand personality

How GANA shows up.

GANA speaks in simple, warm, and clear language. The tone is calm and approachable, reflecting the brand’s direction toward wellness and sustainability.

Fresh

Our colors, imagery, and experiences reflect the freshness of sugarcane.

Grounded

Our identity stays connected to the ingredient, its natural qualities, and where it comes from.

Welcoming

Our spaces and communication feel friendly, comfortable, and easy to understand.

Thoughtful

We consider how each design decision affects the experience, from holding the bottle to understanding its packaging.

Strategic pillars

Five ideas, one fresh experience.

01

Freshness and Wellness

Fresh sugarcane juice is at the center of GANA, with a thoughtful approach to everyday refreshment and clear information about the ingredients.

02

Connection to the Source

The farm story connects the finished drink to where it begins, through named farm sourcing and stories about the ingredient.

03

Sustainability in Practice

Practical choices across sourcing, packaging, and disposal, reducing unnecessary packaging and considering what happens after the drink.

04

Sensory Connection

“Feel the Cane” brings texture into packaging and spaces, “First Press” the sound of preparation, plus scent and taste in store.

05

A Flexible, Recognizable Identity

Logo, typography, colors, and graphic language keep GANA recognizable across flavors, locations, seasons, and applications.

The experience

Feel it.

Packaging

Packaging brings “Feel the Cane” directly into people’s hands. The tall cylindrical bottle and its raised texture translate the form and surface of sugarcane into an everyday object.

Feel the Cane

“Feel the Cane” is GANA’s main tactile expression. The bottle is the primary point of contact. Counters, door handles, carriers, and selected surfaces extend the same idea.

The experience

Hear it. Smell it. Taste it.

First Press

“First Press” connects the sound of preparation to the product. Cane goes in, juice comes out, and you watch it happen.

Scent & taste

A floor-standing diffuser in GANA Green sits near the service area, kept subtle so it rests comfortably alongside the aroma of preparation and the drink itself.

Texture

Inspired by the vertical ridges of sugarcane.

Texture is a core part of GANA’s identity. It connects the bottle, storefront, counter, and door handles, creating a consistent relationship between the ingredient and its surroundings.

Our spaces

Welcoming, from the street in.

Store front

The storefront extends the bottle’s design into architecture. Vertical forms, cane-inspired texture, and the established palette create a visible connection between the product and the place.

Interior

Interior spaces feel fresh, welcoming, and easy to navigate, with clear ordering, preparation, and pickup areas. Textured surfaces are balanced with quieter areas.
