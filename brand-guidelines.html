The brand

We believe freshness begins at the source.

With sugarcane grown on farms, carefully selected, and pressed for people to enjoy. We want that connection to remain visible, in the stories we share, the materials we choose, and the experiences we create.

We bring the feeling of sugarcane into your hands through texture, into our spaces through form, and into the experience through the sound of the first press.

We believe sustainability belongs in everyday decisions: where ingredients come from, how products are packaged, and what happens after the drink is finished.

Why GANA exists

Purpose, audience, positioning.

Fresh sugarcane juice built around freshness, wellness and sustainability, with a direct line back to the ingredient.

A

Purpose

To bring fresh sugarcane juice into everyday life while connecting people to its source through thoughtful design and sensory experiences.

B

Audience

People who care about what they drink and how it is made: students, working professionals, and anyone looking for convenient refreshment, whether they already enjoy sugarcane juice or are trying it for the first time.

C

Positioning

A fresh, contemporary beverage with an elevated yet approachable experience, from farm sourcing to sugarcane-inspired packaging and sensory design.

    Under the hood

    Grid and clear space.

    Construction

    The logo uses a modular grid to organize the letters. Shared alignments, spacing and corner treatments connect every arrangement. The grid is a reference and should not appear in everyday brand applications.

    Clear space

    Keep an open area around every arrangement equal to one letter width. Text, images and borders stay outside it. Measure from the outermost edges and scale it with the logo.

    Incorrect logo use

    Please don’t.

    Consistency depends on preserving the approved artwork.

    • Do not stretch, compress, rotate, or skew the logo.
    • Do not change the letterforms or their rounded corners.
    • Do not alter spacing within an approved arrangement.
    • Do not create additional letter arrangements.
    • Do not recreate the wordmark using Manrope or another typeface.
    • Do not apply unapproved colors, outlines, shadows, or effects.
    • Do not place the logo on a background that obscures its shape.
    • Do not crop the logo or place other elements inside its clear space.

    Texture and pattern

    Feel the cane.

    Raised ridges and recessed grooves, plus a pattern built from the logo forms.

    Repeat a raised vertical ridge and a recessed groove, evenly spaced. Each ridge takes 60% of one repeat, the groove the other 40%. When resizing, scale both together.
