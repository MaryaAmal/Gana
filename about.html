GANA

We believe freshness begins at the source.

GANA is a fresh sugarcane juice brand built around freshness, wellness, and sustainability. It brings sugarcane closer to everyday life through thoughtful packaging, welcoming spaces, and a direct connection to the ingredient.

Our mission

To bring fresh sugarcane juice into everyday life.

We connect people to its source through thoughtful design and sensory experiences.

Our vision

Fresh, simple drinks as an everyday choice.

A world where every sip stays connected to where it came from.

Our voice

Simple, warm, and clear.

GANA speaks in simple, warm, and clear language. The tone is calm and approachable, reflecting the brand’s direction toward wellness and sustainability.

What makes it different

Not another soda.

A fresher choice

We created GANA as a fresh alternative to mass-produced soda. The flavor comes from freshly pressed sugarcane, naturally sweet and kept simple.

Rooted in the ingredient

The identity takes its green color, long vertical form and natural texture from sugarcane itself, from the logo to the bottle to the store.

Felt, not just seen

“Feel the Cane” brings texture into your hands. “First Press” brings in the sound of preparation. Scent and taste complete the experience.

Who it is for

People who care what they drink.

GANA is for people who care about what they drink and how it is made. They value freshness, wellness and more thoughtful choices in their daily routines.
